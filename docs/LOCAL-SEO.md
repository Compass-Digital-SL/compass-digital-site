# Local SEO — what the owner must do (step by step)

This is your checklist for getting Compass Digital found in local and "near me"
searches. The website side is done (Task 17): service-area content, consistent
NAP, an embed-free map link, a reviews section, analytics and verification are
all wired up and read from **WP Admin → Settings → Site Settings**.

What is left is the work only you can do, because it needs your real business
accounts, your phone and your email. Do it in order. Each step is spelled out —
no prior knowledge assumed.

---

## 0. Before you start — decide your NAP and never change it

**NAP = Name, Address, Phone.** Google trusts a business more when its NAP is
*exactly* the same everywhere on the internet. Pick one version now and copy it
the same way every single time.

- **Name:** `Compass Digital`
- **Address:** your real Colombo address, written one way (e.g. `No. 12, Example
  Road, Colombo 05, Sri Lanka`). Decide "No." vs "#", "Road" vs "Rd" now and keep it.
- **Phone:** one number, written one way (e.g. `+94 76 427 8496`).

Write these three lines in a note on your phone. You will paste them many times.

> First, make the website match: go to **WP Admin → Settings → Site Settings**
> and fill in Phone, Email, Address, Service area, and Opening hours. Save.

## 1. Google Business Profile (most important — do this first)

This is the free Google listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in
the box on the right of search results. It is the single biggest local-SEO win.

### 1a. Create or claim it
1. On a computer, open **https://business.google.com** and sign in with the
   Google account you want to own the business (use a business Gmail, not a
   personal one, if you can).
2. Click **Add your business to Google** (or search your business name first in
   case someone already created it — if you find it, click **Claim this business**).
3. **Business name:** type `Compass Digital` exactly (your NAP name).
4. **Business category:** choose `Website designer`. You can add more later
   (e.g. `Software company`, `Marketing agency`, `Internet marketing service`).
5. **Do you have a location customers can visit?**
   - If clients visit your office: choose **Yes**, enter your address exactly
     as your NAP.
   - If you only visit clients / work remotely: choose **No**, then set your
     **service areas** (add Colombo, Kandy, Galle, Jaffna, and "Sri Lanka").
6. **Contact:** enter your phone (your NAP phone) and `https://compassdigital.lk`
   as the website.
7. Click **Finish**.

### 1b. Verify it (this is the step people get stuck on)
Google must confirm the business is really yours. It will offer one or more of:
- **Phone/SMS:** Google sends a code to your phone — type it in. Fastest.
- **Postcard:** Google mails a code to your address (5–14 days). When it
  arrives, log back in and enter the code.
- **Video:** Google asks you to record a short video of your workspace/signage.
Pick whichever it offers, complete it, and wait for the "Verified" tick.

### 1c. Fill the profile out completely (a full profile ranks better)
Once verified, in the profile dashboard set every field you can:
1. **Description:** 2–3 plain sentences. Example: *"Compass Digital is a Sri
   Lankan software and digital services firm in Colombo. We build clear, fast
   websites, SEO, POS systems and custom software for businesses across Sri Lanka."*
2. **Hours:** set your real opening hours (match the website's Site Settings).
3. **Services:** add each of your six services (Website design & development,
   SEO, POS systems, Mobile app development, Ecommerce websites, Custom software).
4. **Photos:** upload at least 5 — your logo, a team photo, your workspace, and
   screenshots of 2–3 projects. Real photos, not stock.
5. **Products / Posts:** optional but good — add a "Post" every few weeks (an
   offer, a new case study, an update). It keeps the profile active.
6. **Messaging:** turn on chat only if you will actually reply within a day.

### 1d. Connect it back to the website
1. On the profile, find the **share link** (the short Google Maps URL for your
   listing) — open your profile on Google Maps, click **Share**, copy the link.
2. Paste it into **WP Admin → Settings → Site Settings → Local & maps → Google
   Maps link**. Save. (This powers the "Open in Google Maps" button.)
3. Find your **reviews link:** on your profile dashboard click **Ask for
   reviews** / **Get more reviews** to copy the short review link. Paste it into
   **Site Settings → Local & maps → Google reviews link**. Save. (This powers the
   "Read our Google reviews" links.)
4. Optional: in Google Maps, right-click your exact pin → the first line is the
   **latitude, longitude**. Paste them into **Site Settings → Latitude /
   Longitude**. Save. (This sharpens your map pin in search.)

## 2. Get reviews (do this continuously, forever)

Reviews are the fuel of local SEO. More real, recent reviews = higher ranking
and more clicks.
1. After every happy project, send the client your **Google reviews link** (the
   one from step 1d-3) by WhatsApp with one line: *"If you were happy with the
   work, a quick Google review would mean a lot — here's the link."*
2. Never buy reviews or write fake ones — Google removes them and can suspend you.
3. **Reply to every review**, good or bad, within a few days. A calm, polite
   reply to a bad review looks better than no reply.
4. Once you have genuine reviews, you may copy the **average rating** (e.g. 4.9)
   and **number of reviews** into **Site Settings → Local & maps**. Only enter
   real Google figures — these feed the star-rating data on the site.

## 3. Bing Places (second search engine, 10 minutes)

1. Open **https://www.bingplaces.com** and sign in with a Microsoft account
   (make one free if needed).
2. Click **New User** → it offers to **import from Google Business Profile** —
   do that; it copies everything, saving you the typing. (Or add manually using
   your exact NAP.)
3. Check every field matches your NAP exactly.
4. Verify the same way (phone/email/postcard) when asked.
5. Publish.

---

## 4. Local directories and citations (consistency is the whole point)

A "citation" is any site that lists your NAP. Google cross-checks them, so every
listing must use the **exact same NAP** from step 0. Do these over a week or two.

For each site below: search for your business first (claim if it exists),
otherwise add it, using your exact NAP, website, hours, and category.

**Sri Lanka directories:**
- Yellow Pages Sri Lanka — https://www.yellowpages.lk
- Lanka Business Directory — https://www.lankabusinessdirectory.lk
- FindMe.lk — https://www.findme.lk
- Sri Lanka Telecom Rainbow Pages — https://www.rainbowpages.lk
- Your Chamber of Commerce listing (e.g. Ceylon Chamber), if you are a member.

**Global directories that help in Sri Lanka:**
- Facebook Page (set the category, address, hours, website) — https://facebook.com
- Instagram business profile (link the website) — https://instagram.com
- LinkedIn Company Page — https://linkedin.com/company
- Apple Business Connect (for Apple Maps) — https://businessconnect.apple.com
- Trustpilot (optional, for reviews) — https://business.trustpilot.com
- Clutch / GoodFirms (good for software/agency leads) — https://clutch.co

**Rule for all of them:** Name, Address, Phone — character for character the
same. If you ever change your phone or move office, update **every** listing and
the website's Site Settings on the same day.

---

## 5. Keep it alive (monthly, 15 minutes)

- Add one Google Business **Post** (offer, update, or new project).
- Ask your two most recent happy clients for a review.
- Reply to any new reviews.
- Check your NAP still matches across Google, Bing, Facebook and the website.
- Glance at Google Business Profile **Insights** and Search Console (Task 16) to
  see what people searched to find you.

## Where each setting lives on the site

| You provide | Where to paste it | What it powers |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Phone, Email, Address, Service area, Hours | Site Settings → Business details | Footer, Contact page, service-area block, schema |
| Google Maps link | Site Settings → Local & maps | "Open in Google Maps" button |
| Google reviews link | Site Settings → Local & maps | "Read our Google reviews" links |
| Latitude / Longitude | Site Settings → Local & maps | Map pin accuracy in schema |
| Average rating / Number of reviews | Site Settings → Local & maps | Star-rating structured data (use real figures only) |
| GA4 Measurement ID | Site Settings → Analytics & verification | Google Analytics (loads after cookie consent) |
| Search Console verification | Site Settings → Analytics & verification | Verifies the site in Search Console |

Analytics only loads after a visitor accepts cookies on the consent bar, and
only once the page is idle, so it never slows the site down.
