# SEO Strategy — Compass Digital

> Strategy for Task 5. Technical implementation lands in Task 16 (Technical SEO) and
> Task 17 (Local SEO and analytics). Voice follows `docs/BRAND.md`: plain, confident, warm.
> Every page's goal is the same conversion — **Request a quote**.

**Base city:** Colombo. **Service area:** all of Sri Lanka, with named-city pushes for
Colombo, Kandy, Galle and Jaffna. Delivery is remote-friendly, so overseas Sri Lankan-owned
businesses are a secondary audience we do not exclude.

## 1. Target audience and their pain points

We sell to owners and managers of small-to-mid Sri Lankan businesses — retail shops,
restaurants and cafes, tour operators, ecommerce brands, and corporates/SMEs. They are
busy, not technical, and have usually been burned once before. In their own words:

1. "Nobody finds us on Google."
2. "Our website looks old and I'm embarrassed to share it."
3. "Billing is slow and messy, and the queue backs up at busy times."
4. "I don't know what's selling and what's just sitting on the shelf."
5. "The developer disappeared after taking the money."
6. "My site is so slow customers leave before it loads."
7. "It doesn't work properly on a phone."
8. "I'm paying for ads but getting no calls."
9. "I can't update the website myself — I have to pay someone for every little change."
10. "Competitors show up above me on Google and I don't know why."
11. "I want to sell online but I don't know where to start."
12. "My POS and my stock don't talk to each other."
13. "I get enquiries on WhatsApp but no proper way to collect orders."
14. "Nobody explains anything in plain language — it's all jargon."
15. "I don't know if the money I spent on my last site did anything."
16. "I need something that works offline when the internet drops."
17. "I have reviews but they're scattered and I can't get more."
18. "I'm scared of being locked into a system I can't get out of."

**What this means for SEO:** our content must answer the question behind each pain
plainly, show proof (real work, real results), and make the next step obviously
"Request a quote."

## 4. Internal linking rules

1. **Hub and spoke.** Services hub links to all 6 service pages; each service links back to
   the hub and to 2–3 relevant industries. Industries hub links to all 5; each industry links
   to the services it needs most (e.g. Restaurants → POS systems, Website, SEO).
2. **Service ↔ industry cross-links.** Every service page lists the industries it serves;
   every industry page lists the services it uses. This builds the *industry + service* map.
3. **Proof links.** Each service and industry page links to at least one relevant Work case
   study; each case study links back to the service(s) and industry it belongs to.
4. **Blog → money pages.** Every Insights post links to the single most relevant service or
   industry page with descriptive anchor text (not "click here"). Pillar posts link to 3+.
5. **Every page → quote.** One primary CTA to `/get-a-quote/` per page, plus contextual
   in-body links where natural. No page is a dead end.
6. **Breadcrumb trail** on all deep pages (Home › Services › SEO) — also drives BreadcrumbList schema.
7. **Descriptive anchors**, keyword-aware but natural. Avoid exact-match stuffing.
8. **Orphan check:** no published page without at least one internal link pointing to it.

## 5. Schema plan (structured data)

Implemented in Task 16; local fields finalised in Task 17. JSON-LD only.

| Schema | Where | Key fields |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Organization** | Site-wide (home) | name, url, logo, sameAs (social), contactPoint |
| **WebSite** | Site-wide (home) | name, url, potentialAction (SearchAction if site search exists) |
| **LocalBusiness** | Home + Contact | name, image, address (Colombo), geo, areaServed: Sri Lanka, telephone, openingHours, priceRange |
| **Service** | Each service page | name, serviceType, provider (Organization), areaServed, hasOfferCatalog |
| **FAQPage** | FAQ section / pages with Q&A | mainEntity: Question + acceptedAnswer |
| **BreadcrumbList** | All deep pages | itemListElement trail |
| **Article** | Each Insights post | headline, author (real person), datePublished, dateModified, image |
| **CreativeWork** | Each case study | name, creator (Organization), about, keywords, result/description |

Rules: one primary entity type per page, no markup for invisible content, validate every
template in Rich Results Test before launch.

## 7. E-E-A-T plan

Compass sells trust, so **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust** are built from
real things, never invented.

- **Real names.** Author bylines on every Insights post with a short real bio and photo.
  An About page with the real team, roles and faces.
- **Real work.** Case studies name the client (with permission), the problem, what we did,
  and the measurable result. No stock mockups passed off as our work.
- **Real results.** Quote actual numbers where the client agrees (e.g. "billing time cut from
  90s to 20s", "organic enquiries up 40% in 4 months"). Where numbers can't be shared, use a
  named client testimonial instead.
- **Contact and trust signals.** Real Colombo address, phone and email in the footer and on
  Contact; consistent NAP everywhere; Google Business Profile linked; privacy and terms pages.
- **Credentials.** Partner/platform badges (e.g. WooCommerce, Meta, Google) only where genuine.
- **Freshness.** `dateModified` kept accurate; review money pages quarterly.
